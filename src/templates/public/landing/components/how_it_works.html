Your Path to Riding

Getting your Washington State motorcycle endorsement is easier than you think

1

Get Your Permit

Visit your local DOL office to take the written knowledge test and obtain your motorcycle instruction permit. Study the Washington Motorcycle Operator Manual.

$25 DOL Fee
2

Enroll at CWMT

Choose from our Basic Rider Course or Intermediate Course. Select a date that works for your schedule - we offer weekend and weekday classes.

2-3 Days
3

Complete Training

Attend classroom sessions and hands-on riding practice. Learn essential skills from certified instructors in a safe, controlled environment.

Expert Instruction
4

Get Endorsed

Pass your skills test at CWMT and receive a completion card. Take it to the DOL to waive the riding test and get your endorsement!

DOL Test Waived

Washington State DOL Approved Training

CWMT is an approved motorcycle training provider by the Washington State Department of Licensing. Upon successful completion of our course, you'll receive a completion card that waives the DOL riding skills test requirement. Simply bring your completion card and instruction permit to any DOL office to receive your motorcycle endorsement.
