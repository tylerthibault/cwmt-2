Quick answers to common questions about our motorcycle training courses

No! We provide all motorcycles for training. You'll learn on safe, well-maintained bikes that are perfect for beginners. All you need to bring is yourself and a positive attitude.

Bring your driver's license, comfortable long pants, closed-toe shoes or boots, a long-sleeved shirt or jacket, and gloves. We provide helmets and all other safety gear. Don't forget water and sunscreen for outdoor sessions!

Our Basic course is 2 days (weekend), Standard course is 3 days, and Premium course is 5 days. Each includes both classroom instruction and hands-on riding practice. You'll receive your certification upon successful completion.

Students must be at least 16 years old with a valid driver's license or learner's permit. Riders under 18 require parental consent. There's no upper age limit – we welcome riders of all ages!

We understand life happens! You can reschedule up to 7 days before your course start date at no charge. Cancellations within 7 days may incur a fee. Contact us as soon as possible if you need to make changes.

Upon successful completion, you'll receive a completion certificate that can be presented at the DMV to waive the riding skills test. You'll still need to pass the written knowledge test at the DMV to receive your motorcycle endorsement.
