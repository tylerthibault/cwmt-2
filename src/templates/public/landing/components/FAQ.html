Everything you need to know about getting your motorcycle endorsement

Yes, you must have a valid Washington State motorcycle instruction permit before attending your first class. You can obtain your permit by visiting any DOL licensing office and passing the written knowledge test. The permit fee is $25 and is separate from the course tuition.

Required items:
  • Valid motorcycle instruction permit
  • Long-sleeved shirt or jacket
  • Long pants (jeans or heavier material)
  • Over-the-ankle boots or sturdy shoes
  • Full-fingered gloves (leather or textile)
  • Eye protection (if not using a full-face helmet)
We provide: DOT-approved helmets and motorcycles for the Basic Rider Course.

The Basic Rider Course is 15+ hours over 2-3 days (typically a weekend format: Friday evening, Saturday and Sunday). The Intermediate Course is 8 hours, usually completed in one day. We offer both weekend and weekday classes to fit your schedule. Specific times will be provided when you enroll.

Upon successful completion of the course, you'll receive a completion card. Take this card along with your instruction permit to any Washington State DOL office to get your motorcycle endorsement added to your driver's license. The completion card waives the DOL riding skills test, so you won't need to take another test at the DOL!

Our certified instructors will teach you everything from the basics of operating a motorcycle to advanced safety techniques. We start with classroom instruction and progress to hands-on riding in a safe, controlled environment. Many of our students have never been on a motorcycle before their first class.

The Basic Rider Course is for new riders or those with limited experience. It includes more classroom time, uses training motorcycles we provide, and covers fundamental riding skills.

The Intermediate Course is for riders who already have some experience and own a motorcycle. It's shorter, focuses on refining your skills, and requires you to bring your own street-legal motorcycle. Both courses culminate in the DOL skills test and provide a completion card for your endorsement.

We maintain small class sizes to ensure personalized attention and safety. Our classes typically have 12-16 students with multiple certified instructors. This ratio allows us to provide individual feedback and ensure everyone gets the practice time they need to succeed.

You must be at least 16 years old to obtain a motorcycle instruction permit in Washington State. Students under 18 must have parental consent to enroll in the course. There is no maximum age limit - we've successfully trained riders of all ages, from teenagers to retirees!

Our instructors are committed to your success, and the vast majority of students pass on their first attempt. If you don't pass the skills evaluation, you may be eligible to retake the course at a reduced rate or schedule additional practice sessions. We'll work with you to identify areas for improvement and help you succeed. Remember, our goal is to make you a safe, confident rider!
