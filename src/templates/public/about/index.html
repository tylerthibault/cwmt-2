About Us

Empowering Riders Since Day One

At Central Washington Motorcycle Training, we're passionate about creating confident, skilled, and safe riders through expert instruction and personalized training.

Our Mission

To provide the highest quality motorcycle training that transforms beginners into confident riders and helps experienced motorcyclists refine their skills. We're committed to creating a safer riding community through expert instruction, modern training methods, and a supportive learning environment.

Our Vision

To be the premier motorcycle training institution in the Pacific Northwest, recognized for excellence in rider education, safety innovation, and community building. We envision a future where every rider has access to world-class training that saves lives and enhances the joy of motorcycling.

Training in Action

Our Story

Central Washington Motorcycle Training was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to exceptional motorcycle training. Founded by experienced riders and certified instructors, we saw a need for a training program that combined safety expertise with the pure joy of riding.

What started as a small operation with a handful of students has grown into a thriving community of riders. We've trained hundreds of students, from complete beginners to experienced motorcyclists looking to sharpen their skills.

Our success comes from our unwavering commitment to quality instruction, state-of-the-art training facilities, and a genuine passion for motorcycling. Every course we teach, every student we train, brings us closer to our goal of making the roads safer for everyone.
