What You'll Learn

Basic Controls & Operation

Master throttle, clutch, braking, and shifting techniques

Safety Techniques

Learn defensive riding and hazard awareness

Maneuvering Skills

Practice turns, curves, and emergency stops

Traffic Strategies

Navigate real-world riding situations confidently

Requirements

  • Valid Washington State motorcycle instruction permit
  • Must be at least 16 years of age
  • Long-sleeved shirt or jacket, long pants, over-the-ankle footwear
  • Full-fingered gloves and eye protection
  • Physical ability to balance and operate a motorcycle
